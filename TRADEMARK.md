# Trademark Policy

Liste's code is open source under the AGPL-3.0. The name **"Liste"**, the Liste logo, and the app icons are trademarks of the Liste maintainers and are **not** licensed under the AGPL. This file explains what you may and may not do with them.

The goal of this policy is narrow: people should be able to tell the official Liste apps and Liste Cloud apart from forks and third-party instances. It is not meant to stop anyone from using, modifying, or self-hosting the software.

## What you may do without asking

- **Self-host Liste** and refer to your instance as "Liste (self-hosted)" or say that it is "powered by Liste".
- **Describe your fork or project** as being based on, derived from, or compatible with Liste, in plain text.
- **Link to** liste.app and to this repository.
- **Use the name** in articles, reviews, tutorials, package descriptions, and other factual references.
- **Keep the name in the source code** you redistribute, including crate names, module paths, identifiers, and this policy.

## What you may not do

- **Present a modified or rebranded fork as Liste.** A redistributed fork must not use "Liste", the logo, or the app icons as its own product name or identity.
- **Publish a fork in an app store or package registry under the name Liste**, or in a way that suggests it is the official app. This includes the App Store, Google Play, the Microsoft Store, Flathub, Homebrew, and similar channels.
- **Use the name or logo in a domain name, product name, or company name** in a way that implies endorsement by or affiliation with the maintainers.
- **Modify the logo or icons** and use the result as your own mark.
- **Offer a hosted service called Liste** or otherwise imply that your hosted instance is Liste Cloud.

## Forks

If you distribute a modified version, please choose a different name and icon. You are encouraged to state that it is derived from Liste. Fixing bugs and submitting them upstream keeps everyone on one codebase, and the maintainers welcome that.

## Questions

If you are not sure whether a use is covered, open a discussion on the repository or email hello@liste.app.
